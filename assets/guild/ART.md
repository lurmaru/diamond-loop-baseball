# 星灯りの旅団の原画

表紙、職業肖像、守護者の3枚を組み込み画像生成で新規に制作しました。背景は不透明です。次の共通指示に、用途ごとの指示をつないで生成し、ゲームに使う大きさに切り出しました。

**共通の画風**

Original illustrated high-fantasy travel journal, atmospheric traditional watercolor, distinctive expressive thin brown pen linework, natural pigment blooms on ivory drawing paper, controlled intricate detail, warm golden light and saturated deep pine green, dusty teal, antique gold, reddish terracotta. Sophisticated Japanese fantasy RPG art direction with grounded anatomy. No words, letters, numbers, captions, logos or typography of any kind.

**表紙**　[valley.webp](valley.webp)

Landscape aspect 3:1 panoramic cover illustration for a fantasy guild management game. A storied guild town of warm timber, red rooftops, cliff stairways, pennants and little lit windows embraced by a dramatic teal mountain valley. A narrow river bends into the distance. A unique remote star beacon tower on the ridge a little RIGHT of center with a single recognizable golden four-point star of light atop it. Warm misty sunrise, luminous ivory gold sky, deep green woodland framing, tiny cloaked traveler at the right foreground entering the town with a lantern. Painterly evocative artisanal landscape; tightly composed richly detailed scene, not a map. The LEFT third of the vista is relatively quiet hazy valley for a subsequent dark text overlay; the village, tower, sunset, and traveler cluster in the right two thirds, important details gracefully weather a shallow crop at top/bottom. Full bleed all edges.
